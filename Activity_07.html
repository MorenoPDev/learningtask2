    My favorite Dish

    Ingredients

    • Chicken
    • Soy Sauce
    • Vinegar
    • Garlic
    • Pepper
    • Dried bay leaves
    • Cooking Oil

    Steps

    1. Marinate: Combine chicken, soy sauce, half the garlic, peppercorns, and bay leaves in a bowl. Marinate for at least 30 minutes (or overnight).
    2. Sear: Heat oil in a large pan over medium-high heat. Remove chicken from the marinade (save the liquid!) and sear the pieces until browned on both sides. Remove chicken and set aside.
    3. Sauté: In the same pan, sauté the remaining garlic until fragrant and lightly golden.
    4. Simmer: Pour in the reserved marinade and water (if using). Bring to a boil, then lower the heat, cover, and simmer for 20 minutes until the chicken is tender.
    5. Add Vinegar: Pour in the vinegar. Do not stir. Let it boil uncovered for 3 to 5 minutes to cook off the harsh acidity.
    6. Reduce & Glaze: Stir in the sugar (if using). Simmer uncovered for another 5 to 10 minutes until the sauce thickens and reduces into a savory glaze.

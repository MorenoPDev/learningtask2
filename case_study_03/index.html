Welcome to Our School

The University of St. La Salle is a premier Catholic institution in Bacolod City, dedicated to teaching minds, touching hearts, and transforming lives. Guided by faith and inspired by the vision of St. John Baptist de La Salle, we form persons of integrity and excellence who are committed to service, innovation, and caring for peoples and the Earth. As a responsive learning community, USLS advances human and Christian education through research, creativity, and social engagement, making a lasting impact in Asia and the Pacific.
